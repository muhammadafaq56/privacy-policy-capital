Academic Notice & Educational Disclaimer

Capital Smart English Notes is an independent educational learning companion authored and compiled by Sir Muhammad Atif (Senior English Specialist, Capital Academy Pasrur) and published by Appix Developers.

This application is developed solely for educational self-study, exam revision, and conceptual grammar clarity. All notes, guides, and exercises represent private pedagogical teaching materials created to assist students in their academic revision.

✔ Summary: Capital Smart English Notes is an offline-first educational study app. We do not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data, accounts, or user identities.

1. Information Collection and Use

Capital Smart English Notes does not require user registration, login, phone numbers, or email addresses to function. You can access all educational materials—including notes, grammar guides, MCQs, and past papers—completely offline without creating an account.

We do not collect any personal identifying information (PII) such as:

  • Your name, address, or contact details
  • Financial or payment information
  • Location data or GPS tracking
  • Audio, camera, or biometric data

2. Device Storage & Local Preferences

The application may store minimal non-personal preferences locally on your device (using standard local storage like SharedPreferences), such as:

  • Bookmark progress (e.g., saved questions or lessons)
  • UI preferences (such as light/dark theme preference)

This information remains exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to any external server.

3. Permissions

The app only requests permissions that are strictly necessary for its educational operations. It does not access sensitive runtime permissions such as camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, or precise location.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Isolation

Capital Smart English Notes operates 100% offline. The application does not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics, tracking tools, or external cloud databases. No personal user data is ever collected, transmitted, or shared with third parties.

5. Children’s Privacy & Educational Use

Our application is designed for students (Classes 9, 10, 11, and 12) and educators. We strictly adhere to student and child privacy standards, including the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy.

We do not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any personal information has inadvertently been provided, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove it.

6. Security

We value your trust. Because our application does not transmit or store personal user information online, your data remains secure on your personal device.
